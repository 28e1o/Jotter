Jotter Version 2.4.0

What's New
- Added redesigned note action menu with support for sharing, exporting, duplicating, clipboard copy, pinning, locking, archiving, and deletion.
- Introduced note info card displaying creation/modification timestamps and word/character counts.
- Implemented one‑tap export to plain text files and clipboard copy for quick sharing.
- Added note duplication to create instant copies of existing notes.
